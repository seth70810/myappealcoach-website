Prepare for Oral Argument

Has oral argument been granted in your case? Congratulations! The Court feels strongly enough about the merits that they want to hear from you in person. But this isn't just about telling your story. Some of the most distinguished jurists in the nation will ask you questions about facts, points of law, and even hypotheticals about your case's effect on future litigants. The resources below link to example oral arguments around the country, and the AI tools will help you organize your record, narrow the issues, build an outline, and anticipate questions from the bench.
